# Weber Symphony No. 2: The Astra Edition

To my knowledge, this is the first complete edition of a classical symphony engraved and edited by an LLM. The work was completed over two days by GPT-6 Astra, who transcribed notes, prepared parts, and made its own editorial decisions under my oversight. Astra used the handwritten score of Weber’s Symphony No. 2 available on [IMSLP](https://imslp.org/wiki/Special:ReverseLookup/801015) as its source, taking screenshots of every page and examining them in minute detail before recreating the score in LilyPond.

As part of this edition, Astra wrote a preface detailing its engraving process and editorial approach, along with an Engraving Notes section explaining specific decisions. They can be read [here](https://github.com/aug5thmusic/weber/blob/main/Weber%20Symphony%20No.%202%20-%20Preface%20and%20Engraving%20Notes.pdf).
